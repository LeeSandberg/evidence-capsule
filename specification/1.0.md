# Evidence Capsule 1.0 alpha

An Evidence Capsule is a portable record of a conclusion and its support. The
format does not assert that a conclusion is true. It makes its support,
assumptions, policy decisions, origin, and revision relationship inspectable.

## Canonical payload

To calculate `integrity.content_hash`, remove the top-level `integrity` field,
serialize the remaining object as UTF-8 JSON with lexicographically sorted keys,
no insignificant whitespace, and `ensure_ascii=false`, then calculate SHA-256.
Prefix the lowercase hexadecimal digest with `sha256:`.

Array order is significant. Producers must establish deterministic ordering
before hashing when their source store does not preserve semantic order.

## Revisions

Revision 1 uses a null or absent `previous_revision_hash`. A later revision sets
`previous_revision_hash` to the complete `integrity.content_hash` of its immediate
predecessor. A correction creates a new revision; it never mutates an already
distributed signed revision.

## Privacy

Evidence may be represented by a permission-scoped reference and content hash.
The format never requires private documents or personal information to be embedded.

## Security status

This alpha defines integrity hashing, not identity authentication. The `signatures`
and `external_anchors` arrays are reserved extension points. Until a signature
profile is standardized and implemented, consumers must not claim that an alpha
capsule cryptographically authenticates its author or reviewer.
